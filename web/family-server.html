Pulsewave Family Server

Your own private music service on your QNAP NAS. Set it up once, and the whole family can listen anywhere.

What you get

  • Your NAS music folder streams to every phone, tablet and computer in the family, with Pulsewave's visuals, lyrics, karaoke and equalizer.
  • Each person gets their own account, with their own favorites and playlists. Anyone can share a playlist with the family.
  • People you allow can add music from their phone or computer. It's saved on the NAS.
  • Lyrics and karaoke timing that anyone adds are shared with everyone.
  • Private: no cloud and no subscription. Your music never leaves your home except to your family's devices.

Set up on the QNAP TS-264 (about 15 minutes)

1Install Container Station

On the NAS, open App Center, search Container Station, and click Install. This lets the NAS run apps like Pulsewave Server. Installing it also creates a shared folder called Container.

2Check your music folder

Open File Station. Pulsewave expects your music in Multimedia/Music, which is the NAS path /share/Multimedia/Music. If your music is somewhere else, for example a share called Music (/share/Music), note that path for step 3. Subfolders are fine. Pulsewave reads everything inside.

Also in File Station, make a folder pulsewave inside the Container share. Pulsewave keeps its accounts and playlists there.

3Create the Pulsewave app

Open Container Station → Applications → Create. Name it pulsewave and paste this:

services:
  pulsewave:
    image: ghcr.io/ryanbarnal/pulsewave-server:latest
    container_name: pulsewave
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "8095:8095"
    environment:
      SERVER_NAME: "Barnal Family Music"
      RESCAN_HOURS: "6"
    volumes:
      - /share/Multimedia/Music:/music
      - /share/Container/pulsewave:/data

If your music is elsewhere, change /share/Multimedia/Music to its path. Change SERVER_NAME to whatever you like. Click Create. The NAS downloads Pulsewave Server and starts it. The first scan of a big music collection can take several minutes.

4Create your admin account

On a computer at home, open http://NAS-IP:8095, for example http://192.168.1.20:8095. You'll find the NAS IP in Qfinder or on the QTS desktop under Control Panel → Network & Virtual Switch. Pulsewave opens and asks you to create the admin account. That's you.

If the page doesn't open, check that QTS's firewall (QuFirewall) allows port 8095.

5Add your family

Tap 🏠 at the top → Add a person. Give each person a name and a starting password. Tick Can add music for anyone you trust to upload songs. Send each person the address, their name and their password. They can change the password themselves under 🏠.

Listening outside the home: Tailscale (free, private)

At home, everyone uses http://NAS-IP:8095. To listen anywhere without opening your router to the internet, use Tailscale. It's a free private network: only devices you invite can reach the NAS.

  1. On the NAS: App Center → search Tailscale → Install → open it and sign in (Google, Microsoft or Apple account).
  2. At login.tailscale.com/admin/machines, find the NAS. Note its name, for example ts-264, or its 100.x.y.z address.
  3. To invite family: click ⋯ → Share… next to the NAS and send the invite to each person's email. On the free plan you can also add up to 3 people to your own Tailscale network instead.
  4. Each family member installs the Tailscale app (iPhone, Android, Windows or Mac), signs in, accepts the invite, and leaves Tailscale switched on.
  5. Everyone then opens http://ts-264:8095 (or http://100.x.y.z:8095) from anywhere: home Wi-Fi, mobile data or travel.
Prefer a public link instead, like https://music.ryanbarnal.com, with no Tailscale app for family? That's possible with a free Cloudflare Tunnel. Your login page is then on the open internet, protected by passwords and lock-out after 10 wrong tries. Ask and it can be added.

Using it on phones, tablets and computers

DeviceHow
iPhone / iPadOpen the server address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app.
AndroidOpen it in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Windows / Mac browserJust open the address and bookmark it.
Pulsewave desktop appTap 🏠 → enter the server address → sign in.

In 🏠 you can switch between the family library and the device's own library at any time. Device-only songs aren't affected.

The pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com website can't connect to the home server directly, because browsers block plain http:// servers on secure https:// pages. Open the server's own address instead. It has the full Pulsewave app built in.

Looking after it

  • New music: copy files into the music folder on the NAS. Pulsewave picks them up within 6 hours, or tap 🏠 → Rescan music folder to see them right away. Songs the family adds from the app go into Music/Pulsewave Uploads/<name>.
  • Album art and lyrics: taken from the files, or from a cover.jpg / folder.jpg in the album folder, or from a .lrc file next to a song with the same name.
  • Removing a song: as admin, use ⋯ → Remove from family library. This hides it for everyone. Pulsewave never deletes your files. Restore removed songs brings them back.
  • Forgotten password: as admin, use 🏠 → Reset password next to that person.
  • Updating: in Container Station, open pulsewave → Action → Recreate, or remove and create it again with the same settings. Accounts and playlists are kept in Container/pulsewave.
  • Backup: include the Container/pulsewave folder in your NAS backup (Hybrid Backup Sync).

Privacy & security

  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes (scrypt), never in plain text. Sign-ins last 180 days per device.
  • After 10 wrong passwords, that address is locked out for 15 minutes.
  • Pulsewave Server never changes or deletes files in your music folder. It only adds new uploads.
  • With Tailscale, the server isn't reachable from the public internet at all.
