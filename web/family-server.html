Pulsewave Family Server

Your own private music service on your QNAP NAS. Set it up once, and the whole family can listen anywhere.

What you get

  • Your NAS music folder streams to every phone, tablet and computer in the family, with Pulsewave's visuals, lyrics, karaoke and equalizer.
  • Each person gets their own account, with their own favorites and playlists. Anyone can share a playlist with the family.
  • People you allow can add music from their phone or computer. It's saved on the NAS.
  • Lyrics and karaoke timing that anyone adds are shared with everyone.
  • Private: no cloud and no subscription. Your music never leaves your home except to your family's devices.

Set up on the QNAP TS-264 (about 15 minutes)

1Install Container Station

On the NAS, open App Center, search Container Station, and click Install. This lets the NAS run apps like Pulsewave Server. Installing it also creates a shared folder called Container.

2Check your music folder

Open File Station. Pulsewave expects your music in Multimedia/Music, which is the NAS path /share/Multimedia/Music. If your music is somewhere else, for example a share called Music (/share/Music), note that path for step 3. Subfolders are fine. Pulsewave reads everything inside.

Also in File Station, make a folder pulsewave inside the Container share. Pulsewave keeps its accounts and playlists there.

3Create the Pulsewave app

Open Container Station → Applications → Create. Name it pulsewave and paste this:

services:
  pulsewave:
    image: ghcr.io/ryanbarnal/pulsewave-server:1.6.1
    container_name: pulsewave
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "8095:8095"            # at home: http://NAS-IP:8095
    environment:
      SERVER_NAME: "Barnal Family Music"
      PUBLIC_URL: "https://media.ryanbarnal.com"
      TRUST_PROXY: "1"         # visitors arrive through Cloudflare; use their real address for lock-outs
      RESCAN_HOURS: "6"
    volumes:
      # your music folder (Pulsewave never deletes or changes your files;
      # songs the family adds go into a "Pulsewave Uploads" folder inside it)
      - /share/Multimedia/Music:/music
      # accounts, playlists, favorites, listen links, lyrics and cover-art cache
      - /share/Container/pulsewave:/data

  # Publishes the server securely at https://media.ryanbarnal.com, with no router changes
  tunnel:
    image: cloudflare/cloudflared:2026.10.7
    container_name: pulsewave-tunnel
    restart: unless-stopped
    command: tunnel --no-autoupdate run
    environment:
      TUNNEL_TOKEN: "PASTE-YOUR-CLOUDFLARE-TUNNEL-TOKEN-HERE"
    depends_on:
      - pulsewave

If your music is elsewhere, change /share/Multimedia/Music to its path. Change SERVER_NAME to whatever you like. Leave TUNNEL_TOKEN as it is for now. You'll paste it in step 7. (Until then the pulsewave-tunnel app will show as stopped or restarting, and that's expected.) Click Create. The NAS downloads Pulsewave Server and starts it. The first scan of a big music collection can take several minutes.

4Create your admin account

On a computer at home, open http://NAS-IP:8095, for example http://192.168.1.20:8095. You'll find the NAS IP in Qfinder or on the QTS desktop under Control Panel → Network & Virtual Switch. Pulsewave opens and asks you to create the admin account. That's you.

If the page doesn't open, check that QTS's firewall (QuFirewall) allows port 8095.

5Add your family

Tap 🏠 at the top → Add a person. Give each person a name and a starting password. Tick Can add music for anyone you trust to upload songs. Send each person the address, their name and their password. They can change the password themselves under 🏠.

Your public address: media.ryanbarnal.com (Cloudflare Tunnel)

This lets the family use Pulsewave from anywhere, right on pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com, with nothing to install and no router changes. Cloudflare securely connects https://media.ryanbarnal.com to your NAS.

6Create the tunnel in Cloudflare

  1. Sign in at dash.cloudflare.com and open Zero Trust in the left menu. The first time, pick a team name and the Free plan. It may ask for a card, but the Free plan costs nothing.
  2. Go to Networks → Tunnels → Create a tunnel → choose Cloudflared → name it pulsewave → Save.
  3. On "Install and run a connector", choose Docker. You'll see a command ending in --token eyJh…. Copy only the long token after --token.
  4. Click Next, then add a Public hostname:
    Subdomain media · Domain ryanbarnal.com · Type HTTP · URL pulsewave:8095 → Save tunnel. Cloudflare creates the DNS record for you.

7Paste the token on the NAS

In Container Station → Applications → pulsewave, edit the YAML. Replace PASTE-YOUR-CLOUDFLARE-TUNNEL-TOKEN-HERE with your token, keeping the quotes, then save and recreate the app. Within a minute the tunnel shows Healthy in Cloudflare.

8Try it

On your phone with Wi-Fi off, open https://media.ryanbarnal.com. Pulsewave should open. Then open pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com. The Library shows Sign in to Barnal Family Music.

Keep the token secret. Anyone with it could run your tunnel. If it leaks, delete the tunnel in Cloudflare and make a new one.

How your family listens

With an account (full experience)

They open pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com → Sign in to Barnal Family Music → enter the name and password you gave them. They get the whole family library, their own favorites and playlists, playlists others shared, lyrics and karaoke. On a phone, use Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.

With a listen link (no account)

Open any of your playlists → ⋯ → Make a listen link. The link is copied, ready to paste into Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp or email. Anyone who opens it can play that playlist only on pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com, with lyrics and visuals, without signing in. The link always plays your latest version of the playlist. Use ⋯ → Turn off listen link to stop it at any time.

Optional: private access with Tailscale instead

If you'd rather not have a public address, skip the Cloudflare steps and use Tailscale. It's a free private network: only devices you invite can reach the NAS, but each family member must install the Tailscale app, and listen links won't work for people outside it.

  1. On the NAS: App Center → search Tailscale → Install → open it and sign in (Google, Microsoft or Apple account).
  2. At login.tailscale.com/admin/machines, find the NAS. Note its name, for example ts-264, or its 100.x.y.z address.
  3. To invite family: click ⋯ → Share… next to the NAS and send the invite to each person's email. On the free plan you can also add up to 3 people to your own Tailscale network instead.
  4. Each family member installs the Tailscale app (iPhone, Android, Windows or Mac), signs in, accepts the invite, and leaves Tailscale switched on.
  5. Everyone then opens http://ts-264:8095 (or http://100.x.y.z:8095) from anywhere: home Wi-Fi, mobile data or travel.

Using it on phones, tablets and computers

DeviceHow
iPhone / iPadOpen the server address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app.
AndroidOpen it in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
Windows / Mac browserJust open the address and bookmark it.
Pulsewave desktop appTap 🏠 → enter the server address → sign in.

In 🏠 you can switch between the family library and the device's own library at any time. Device-only songs aren't affected.

With the Cloudflare address set up, pulsewave.ryanbarnal.com connects to your server directly. Without it (home-only or Tailscale), open the server's own address instead. Browsers block plain http:// servers on secure https:// pages, but the server has the full Pulsewave app built in.

Looking after it

  • New music: copy files into the music folder on the NAS. Pulsewave picks them up within 6 hours, or tap 🏠 → Rescan music folder to see them right away. Songs the family adds from the app go into Music/Pulsewave Uploads/<name>.
  • Album art and lyrics: taken from the files, or from a cover.jpg / folder.jpg in the album folder, or from a .lrc file next to a song with the same name.
  • Removing a song: as admin, use ⋯ → Remove from family library. This hides it for everyone. Pulsewave never deletes your files. Restore removed songs brings them back.
  • Forgotten password: as admin, use 🏠 → Reset password next to that person.
  • Updating: in Container Station, open Applications → pulsewave → Edit, change the version at the end of the image: ghcr.io/ryanbarnal/pulsewave-server:… line to the newest one, then Update. Accounts and playlists are kept in Container/pulsewave.
  • Backup: include the Container/pulsewave folder in your NAS backup (Hybrid Backup Sync).

Privacy & security

  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes (scrypt), never in plain text. Sign-ins last 180 days per device.
  • After 10 wrong passwords, that address is locked out for 15 minutes.
  • Pulsewave Server never changes or deletes files in your music folder. It only adds new uploads.
  • Through Cloudflare, every connection is encrypted (https). Your home IP address stays hidden and no router ports are opened.
  • Because the sign-in page is on the internet, give everyone a password of at least 8 characters that they don't use anywhere else. Pulsewave requires 8 or more.
  • Listen links are long random codes that can't be guessed. They only open the one playlist, never your whole library, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • Cloudflare's free plan is meant for normal family use like this. Streaming music to your family is fine. Very heavy use, such as sharing video publicly, isn't what it's for.
  • With Tailscale instead, the server isn't reachable from the public internet at all.
