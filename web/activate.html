Activate Pulsewave

Desktop app: 30 days free

The Windows and Mac app is free for 30 days. After that it asks for an activation key. One key works on all your computers.

How to get a key

Ask Ryan Ocbina Barnal, who made Pulsewave, for your key.

Then open the desktop app → Settings (gear icon) → Activation, paste the key and press Activate. Or paste it on the screen that appears when the trial ends.

Free forever: the web app

Pulsewave in your browser and on your phone stays free, with no trial and no key. Open the web app
