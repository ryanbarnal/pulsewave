Quick help

  • Add music: press Add music or drag audio files onto the window.
  • Visual effects: pick Bars, Wave, Radial, Galaxy or Tunnel. The colored dots change the theme.
  • Equalizer: open the Equalizer tab, drag the 10 sliders or choose a preset.
  • Library & playlists: songs you add are saved on this device. In Library, browse Playlists, Songs, Artists and Albums. Tap New Playlist to make one, or use ⋯ on any song → Add to playlist.
  • Lyrics: appear over the visuals. They come from a matching .lrc file, the song file itself, or an online search. You can also paste them in the Lyrics tab. Press Lyrics or L to switch between lyrics and visual effects.
  • Full-screen visuals: press ⛶ or F.
