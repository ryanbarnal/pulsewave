    Quick help

    • Add music: press Add music or drag audio files onto the window.
    • Visual effects: pick Bars, Wave, Radial, Galaxy or Tunnel. The colored dots change the theme.
    • Equalizer: open the Equalizer tab, drag the 10 sliders or choose a preset.
    • Full-screen visuals: press ⛶ or F.
